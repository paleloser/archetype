# CM channels

The channels `/cm` drafts for. Edit freely: add a channel, change a locale, or tighten a
rule. Every draft is written for one of these, in its locale. `/product-setup` fills this in,
and `/cm` asks for it while it's empty. Limits marked *(verify)* come from general knowledge
of the platform and weren't checked against its current rules. Correct them when you know
better.

**Audience comes from the topic, not the channel.** A topic for the customer goes to every
channel where customers are; each channel below only changes the format, unless it says its
audience is fixed.

**Videos come in two cuts**, shot separately: vertical 9:16 for Reels/Shorts/Stories, and
horizontal 16:9 for LinkedIn and anywhere else a video goes. Ask for both in the same brief.
Videos use the app's dark theme and have no on-screen text.
